Currently this fork is verified working on the C5 modules.
I have not worked on backward compatibility at all, yet. That's coming.
Note: This is shaping up to be an interesting little crowd simulator.
Simulates varying strength devices at varying repeat intervals with drift.
Plan to add the ability to add a 'mute' switch to one of the I/O pins to stop TX.
This runs much more tame than an absolute flood of random IDs.
I have mostly coded this with Claude. Took the, "I want to learn" approach with it.
  The whole thing has been very interesting and I'm actually picking up a little bit of C.
